About Us

We believe that the health of people, animals, and the environment are deeply connected. Our program is built to foster interdisciplinary collaboration among students, with a focus on

Breaking Down Silos:
Uniting medicine, veterinary science, environmental science, and public health into a single problem-solving framework.
Proactive Prevention:
Training students to address global challenges like zoonoses, pollution, and antimicrobial resistance before they escalate.
Interactive Mastery:
Utilizing case-based learning, disease spread simulations, and real-world data analytics to turn theory into practice.

FAQ

Who is the intended audience?

High school and undergraduate students interested in how human, animal, and environmental health connect — plus the educators and mentors supporting them.

See the Curriculum Modules page for the full roadmap of each track.

How are interactive games used?
  • Live Data Dashboard: Track real-time disease surveillance.
  • Outbreak Simulations: Act as an epidemiologist making active containment tradeoffs.
  • Resource Games: Strategize and allocate budgets to prevent the next global pandemic.
