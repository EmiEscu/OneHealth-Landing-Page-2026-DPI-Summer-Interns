UnderGrad
Lab

One Health

  1. Intro to One Health

    Explore how human, animal, and environmental health are connected, and test your knowledge in an interactive disease outbreak simulation.

  2. Data, Dashboards, and Health Equity

    Analyze interactive data dashboards to uncover hidden health inequities and discover how AI can responsibly track disease outbreaks across communities.

  3. Values & Ethics

    Analyze the social and ethical impacts of disease outbreaks by exploring how communication, stigma, and misinformation shape community trust and public health responses.

  4. Systems Thinking

    Master systems thinking by mapping feedback loops, identifying leverage points, and predicting unintended consequences in complex One Health scenarios.

  5. Capstone Project

Career Advantages of One Health

Understanding how human, animal, and environmental systems connect gives you a massive competitive advantage in the modern job market.

  • High Earning Potential:

    Interdisciplinary roles in health data, epidemiology, and management offer median salaries from $84,000 to $118,000 with rapid projected growth.

  • Standout Resume:

    Top employers across healthcare, consulting, and tech actively seek graduates who can analyze complex systems and solve multifaceted problems.

  • Workplace Readiness:

    Training in cross-system logic makes it easier to communicate across disciplines, lead diverse teams, and adapt to shifting industry demands.
